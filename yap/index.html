05 / A few things to know

Good questions.

What can we talk about?

Almost anything: life, ideas, relationships, philosophy, tech, culture, travel, interesting hypotheticals, or whatever's on your mind. The conversation is informal and collaborative.

Do I need to come with a specific problem?

Nope. You can arrive with a topic or just say hello. We'll take it from there.

Is this therapy or professional coaching?

No. It's a personal conversation and exchange of perspectives. I don't present it as therapy, mental-health treatment, or regulated professional advice.

Can I do audio only?

Yes. You're welcome to join the call without your camera.

How do I book?

Use the booking button to choose an available time on Calendly. The booking page will provide the details for your session.

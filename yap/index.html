04 / Good questions

Before we talk

What can we talk about?

Almost anything: life decisions, ideas, relationships, philosophy, tech, culture, travel, interesting hypotheticals, or whatever's on your mind. The conversation is informal and collaborative.

Do I need to come with a specific problem or agenda?

Not at all. You can arrive with a specific question or just say hello and see where the conversation goes.

Is this therapy or professional coaching?

No. It's a personal conversation and exchange of perspectives. It is not therapy, mental-health treatment, or regulated professional advice.

Can I join audio-only with my camera off?

Yes—you're completely welcome to join with camera on or audio-only, whichever helps you talk most comfortably.

How does scheduling work?

Click any booking button to pick an available slot on Calendly in your local time zone. Your confirmation and Google Meet link arrive right away.

Not at all. You're welcome to bring a question or a topic, but spontaneous conversations are welcome too.

No. This is a conversational experience and a space to exchange perspectives, not a substitute for therapy, medical care, or professional financial or legal advice.

Yes. You can use video or join audio-only.

06 / About Nick

Curiosity is a way of paying attention

I’m a communicator, curious traveler, and lifelong question-asker. I like exploring ideas out loud: following a thought, testing an assumption, and listening for the part I hadn’t considered yet.

This is the human side of that practice: a place to investigate what matters, share what I’m learning, and connect with people who enjoy a genuine, thoughtful conversation.

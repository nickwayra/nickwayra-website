06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Do I need to be fluent already?

No, but you should know enough basic English to participate in a simple conversation. The sessions are designed primarily for learners who want additional speaking practice.

Will we study grammar?

Our focus is conversational confidence. I can highlight useful grammar or phrasing when it naturally comes up, rather than using a rigid textbook curriculum.

What if I don't know what to talk about?

That's fine. I can offer topics and questions based on your interests.

How do sessions work?

Pick a convenient slot on Calendly. You'll receive the meeting details and can join online.

Will I become fluent after one session?

Fluency takes consistent practice. A session can provide useful speaking time and feedback, but no particular outcome is guaranteed.

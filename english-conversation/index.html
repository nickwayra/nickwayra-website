04 / Good questions

Before we talk

What English level do I need?

These sessions are best for intermediate learners who can hold at least a basic conversation in English and want to speak more naturally and confidently. Absolute beginners may be better served by a structured introductory grammar course first.

Will you correct my mistakes?

Yes, thoughtfully. The goal is to keep you speaking comfortably while giving you useful, natural alternatives. Just let me know if you prefer more frequent correction or more uninterrupted flow.

Can we talk about my work, travel, or personal interests?

Absolutely. Career topics, upcoming travel, daily life, technology, culture, or anything you enjoy—practicing subjects you actually care about is the whole point.

Should I start with the 30-minute or 60-minute session?

If it's our first time speaking, start with the $10 30-minute Intro Session. Once you're comfortable with the format, you can book 60-minute sessions anytime.

How do the sessions take place?

Pick a time on Calendly using the booking buttons on this page. You'll automatically receive a Google Meet link for our online session.

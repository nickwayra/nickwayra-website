05 / Before we talk

Good questions.

What English level do I need?

You should be able to hold at least a basic conversation in English. If you know some English but struggle to speak confidently, these sessions are designed with you in mind.

Will you correct my mistakes?

Yes, thoughtfully. The goal is to keep you speaking comfortably while providing useful feedback. Tell me if you'd prefer more frequent correction or more uninterrupted conversation.

Is this a formal English class?

No. It's personal conversation practice, not an accredited class or test-preparation program. We can focus on areas you want to improve, but the format is discussion-led.

Can we talk about my job or interests?

Absolutely. Work, travel, everyday life, technology, culture, or anything you enjoy. Practicing topics you care about is the point.

How do the sessions work?

Choose an available time using the Calendly booking links. You'll receive your meeting details through the booking process. Sessions take place online.

Should I choose the 30- or 60-minute option?

Start with 30 minutes if you'd like to meet and try the format first. Choose an hour if you're ready to spend more time practicing your English.
